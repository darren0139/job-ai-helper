# Governed capability research sufficiency v2

This extends the existing `possible_new_capability` research interpreter and
maintenance Human Review screen. Technology identity and relationship research
retain their separate contracts. No scoring or knowledge publication occurs.

## Support contract

All of these must have exact, source-backed retrieved-document evidence:

- bounded definition;
- boundaries and responsibilities;
- inclusions;
- exclusions / non-proving examples;
- observable implementation evidence predicates.

Support requires one of: a governed normative source; at least two independent
strong sources converging on definition and boundaries; or a strong defining
source plus independent boundary, responsibility, predicate and exclusion
corroboration. This grants eligibility for human review only. All fields must
still be present and all conflict/currentness/overlap checks must pass.

Source governance reuses `source_authority_registry_v1.json`. Legacy definition
scopes remain normative. New technical scopes require reviewed document paths,
review provenance, source kind and an explicit origin identity. The eight new
scopes are based on the saved calibration audit and are reviewable policy
changes; they do not approve any capability or relationship. They are
subject-scoped, not blanket vendor authority. Unknown sources, references and
provider authority labels never create authority.

Shared ownership, known mirrors and syndication must share an origin ID in the
registry. Identical subject-bound document text is additionally deduplicated
even across declared origins. Novel paraphrased mirrors still require accurate
reviewed origin metadata; this is not an automated publisher-authentication
service.

## Deterministic association and limitations

Only paragraphs containing the subject, or the single paragraph immediately
following a subject heading, can contribute. Blank lines, omitted passages and
new headings bound association. Definition heads remain subject-specific;
adjacent responsibility, inclusion, exclusion and predicate statements can
contribute within the same block. Negation and qualifiers remain in exact text
diagnostics. Evidence retains URL, governed origin, paragraph fingerprint and
raw evidence fingerprint.

Search snippets are tentative diagnostics, not sufficient document evidence.
The native Tavily Search adapter now requests raw document text for this route.
No result is sufficient without the required fields. Deterministic contradiction
checks cover explicit opposing propositions and unresolved definition-token
convergence; they do not claim exhaustive natural-language understanding.

## Explicit escalation and currentness

Native bulk planning passes current saved evidence into research planning.
Plans choose one missing-evidence query, can follow explicit primary references,
or request a reviewed document whose full text is missing. References must be
retrieved and evaluated independently. Each capability query permits one
provider attempt, with the existing three-round ceiling and bulk budget.
Sufficient results plan zero additional calls. Exhausted research stays blocked.

The interpretation and plan versions were bumped. Existing saved interpretations
must be refreshed explicitly through the existing maintenance action; their raw
evidence and request IDs are retained. Rendering diagnostics performs no fetch,
refresh, draft creation, approval or publication. Draft fields, regression
preview, human review and publication remain existing separate actions.

## Calibration

The five saved calibration results retain exact original candidate IDs in the
offline report. All remain `research_more`: their saved evidence is search
snippets, and exclusions/predicates remain incomplete. Microservices, cloud
security and NAC now contribute provisional support from reviewed scopes;
data analysis remains unsupported for its exact display scope, and distributed
systems needs bounded primary/document evidence. Passing all five is not a goal.
The compact test fixture preserves raw source snippets, request IDs and original
candidate IDs without importing local database state.
